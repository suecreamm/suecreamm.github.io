This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site and to improve its content.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

* pages visited
* approximate geographic location
* device and browser information
* referral source
* general interactions with the website

## Analytics consent

When you accept analytics, Google Analytics may use analytics cookies to measure visits and sessions and to distinguish between users.

If you decline analytics, analytics cookies are not stored or accessed. However, limited cookieless measurement signals may still be sent to Google through Google Consent Mode. These signals do not use Analytics cookies or persistent Analytics identifiers and may be used by Google for measurement and modeling.

Your consent choice is stored locally in your browser so that the website can remember your preference.

This website does not use Google Analytics for personalized advertising. Advertising-related storage, user data, and personalization remain disabled.

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Information processed through Google Analytics is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and data-processing practices.
